Celtic Tree Calendar

The Ogham Tree Year
ᚁ ᚂ ᚅ

Discover the tree that rules your birth. The Celtic Tree Calendar gives a tree, and an ogham letter, to each of thirteen months of the year, every one with its own character, folklore and meaning.

A complete, ground-up rebuild of the original app, pairing brand-new hand-painted botanical artwork with genuine ogham scholarship.

Features

  • A unique hand-painted botanical illustration for every one of the thirteen trees
  • Today — your current tree with its ogham letter, dates and reading
  • The Celtic Tree Year — an interactive wheel you can spin through all thirteen signs
  • Trees — a gallery of the original botanical illustrations
  • Deep lore for each sign — meaning, mythology, folklore, traditional woodland uses, and the reading for those born in its time
  • Authentic ogham — each sign’s word-ogham kenning, and the medieval colour-ogham and bird-ogham
  • Home-screen widgets — the tree of the day, in small and medium sizes
  • Share a beautiful card of any tree
  • An optional daily reminder of the tree of the day

About the Tradition

We tell the tradition straight. The tree calendar was devised by the poet Robert Graves in 1948, not by the ancient druids. But the trees, their ogham letters, and their folklore are genuine, and we cite our sources throughout.

Privacy

No ads. No tracking. No subscriptions. No in-app purchases. No network calls. Everything is computed on your device and works entirely offline.
